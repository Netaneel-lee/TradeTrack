TradeTrack V3 - Installable Android PWA

1. Put this folder on a web host that supports HTTPS (GitHub Pages is a free option).
2. Open the hosted site in Chrome on Android.
3. Tap the browser menu (three dots).
4. Choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
5. Confirm. TradeTrack will appear on the phone like an app.

Important:
- PWA installation requires HTTPS when hosted online (localhost is an exception for development).
- The current journal stores trades locally on the device/browser.
- Google Play publishing is a separate later step and may require a Google Play developer account.
